What you'd be getting into

Knox Lives is a small Project Zomboid server (Build 42) where one world runs for months. The power fails, the Army seals the county, and a few dozen people at most rebuild it town by town. It is slow, social and dangerous, and it remembers you.

Your first evening

1

You arrive in Muldraugh

Everyone starts there, at a place that fits their job: police at the station, medics at the clinic. You have what your story gives you.

2

Four hours of shelter

For your first four hours of play nobody can hurt you, and you can't hurt anyone. Learn the streets.

3

Know where to run

Muldraugh's community centre is safe ground. Nobody can hurt anyone inside. After that, fighting is on across the open county.

That is all you need on day one. Everything else arrives as the county reaches it.

The county opens a piece at a time

  1. Season ZeroMuldraugh, then three southern towns. Survive, win a second community centre, bring the first lights back.
  2. The river roadThe lake and the river towns. New arrivals, the first town kept lit, farms that matter.
  3. The western roadsFarm country and the county seat. Long trips, water restored, fuel grown on farms.
  4. The highway northThe last town before the city, then Louisville itself.

Each chapter opens when the county has earned it, not on a timer. Beyond the open towns stands the Army's line: you will be warned three times.

The rules, in short

Part of the game

  • Fighting on open ground
  • Robbery, ambushes, theft from shops: crimes, answered by other players
  • Raids and fire in booked disputes and the Badlands
  • Lying, spying, smuggling

Never: it ends your stay

  • Breaking or burning what isn't yours
  • Breaking into a claimed home, by any trick
  • Attacking people on safe ground or new arrivals
  • Exploits, cheats, logging out to escape a fight
  • Harassment

The full rules, the County Charter, are pinned on Discord. Short version first, detail after.

What it asks of you

  • A few evenings a week, for many weeks. A county this small needs people who are seen.
  • Playing a character, not a loot run. A villain is welcome; cruelty to the person behind the character is not.
  • Losing well. A fight lost fairly is part of your story.
  • A decent PC: 16 GB of memory, a 4 GB graphics card, the game on an SSD. The mods are about a 3 GB download.

The trial run

Before the county opens for good, a three-week trial runs with testers. Progress is wiped at the end. If you'd like to help find what breaks, tick "trial run" when you apply, and send bugs and feedback through Discord.
