Knox County, Kentucky · July 1993

Knox Lives

A family of Project Zomboid projects about one idea: the county keeps a record of the people in it. Who they were, what they made, how long they lasted, and how their file closed.

FOUR PROJECTS · ONE COUNTY · BUILD 42

The projects

THE MOD

Knox Lives

A full overhaul of the game's interface: a file on the survivor, kept by a distant observer. The licence, the clothes on the desk, the medical record, the map under glass, the journal, for her or for him. Everything wears as the days go by.

in development · 1.0
THE CRAFT

Knox Lives: Set in Stone

Deeper knapping and masonry: stone that matters, quality that lasts, blocks dressed true, floors and walls that weather and wear where people walk, a hearth and an oven that hold the fire's heat. Traps, slings, cairns and gabions. Works alone or beside Knox Lives, which writes what you made into the file.

in testing · 0.15
THE SERVER

Knox Lives: the county

A small, vetted multiplayer server. One world that runs for months, power restored town by town, trades that need each other, outlaws answered in the world, and a record of everyone who lived there. What you'd be getting into.

auditing and vetting
THE PARTY LINE

Knox Lives on Discord

Where the county talks: applications, notices, the morning card, quiet death notices, and the game's chat. One bot, County Records, ties it to the server.

being set up

What ties them together

The record

Every project writes into the same idea of a file: the mod keeps one character's, Set in Stone adds what they made, the server keeps everyone's.

One county, one year

Knox County in July 1993. Nothing appears that couldn't exist there.

Third person, never "you"

The record speaks about people, never at them. Heroes and villains alike.
